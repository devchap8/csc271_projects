A SMARTER SPACED REPETITION SYSTEM

Understand your words. Not just recognize them.

Spaced repetition is the most effective way to make new vocabulary stick - but most SRS apps freeze every word into a single sentence you'll eventually memorize by shape, not meaning. CardWell pulls a fresh, real example every time you review, so you actually understand the word in context.

WHY SPACED REPETITION

The most reliable way to make a word stick

Spaced repetition has been the backbone of serious language self-study for over a decade, and for good reason - it schedules review at the exact moment you're about to forget, so the memory gets reinforced instead of relearned from scratch.

Backed by memory research

Reviewing right before you'd naturally forget strengthens long-term recall far better than cramming.

Efficient use of study time

You only review what's about to fade, instead of re-reading everything every day.

Scales with your library

Whether you have 50 words or 5,000, the schedule adapts to keep everything fresh.
WHERE TYPICAL SRS APPS FALL SHORT

Same sentence, every time - that's a memorization shortcut, not understanding

When you always see the exact sentence you first saved, your brain learns to recognize the sentence, not the word. Many words also carry several meanings that one saved sentence can't show.

Typical SRS apps

  • Same example sentence shown forever
  • Easy to "autopilot" past the sentence without reading it
  • Only one meaning or context per word
  • Your cards stay locked in your own local library

CardWell

  • A new real sentence pulled from the shared database each review
  • Forces you to actually process the word in context, every time
  • Multiple meanings surface naturally over repeated reviews
  • Your cards join a pool that helps every other learner too
HOW IT WORKS

From one sentence to a whole shared library

  1. Save a card from anything

    Pull a sentence with a new word from a show, podcast, or book you're already studying - just like any SRS app.

  2. It joins the shared pool

    Your sentence becomes one of many for that word, contributed by learners studying the same language.

  3. Review in a new context

    Every time the word comes due, CardWell pulls a different sentence - so you build real understanding, not sentence recall.
