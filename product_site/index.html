WHY SPACED REPETITION

The most reliable way to make a word stick

Spaced repetition has been the number one vocab learning tool for serious language self-study for over a decade. It schedules reviews at the exact moment you're about to forget, gradually integrating words into your long-term memory.

Backed by memory research

Reviewing right before you'd naturally forget strengthens long-term recall far better than cramming or random reviews.

Efficient use of study time

Review what you need to on a smart algorithmic schedule, instead of re-reading everything every day.

Scales with your library

Whether you have 50 words or 5,000, the schedule adapts to keep everything fresh.
WHERE TYPICAL SRS APPS FALL SHORT

Memorizing the sentence shape instead of understanding the word

When you always see the exact sentence you first saved, your brain learns to recognize the sentence, not the word. Many words also carry several meanings and nuances that one sentence alone can't show.

Typical SRS apps

  • Same example sentence shown forever
  • Easy to autopilot past the sentence without reading it
  • Only one meaning or context per word
  • Your cards stay locked in your own local library

CardWell

  • A new real sentence pulled from the shared database each review
  • Forces you to actually process the word in context, every time
  • Multiple meanings surface naturally over repeated reviews
  • Your cards join a pool that helps every other learner too
HOW IT WORKS

From one sentence to a whole shared library

  1. Save a card from anything

    Pull a sentence with a new word from a show, podcast, or book you're already studying. Create cards manually, or with tools like Yomitan or Migaku.

  2. It joins the shared pool

    Your sentence is stored with others containing the same word, contributed by learners studying the same language.

  3. Review in a new context

    When the word is due, CardWell pulls a different sentence. You build real understanding, not sentence recall.
